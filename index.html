WELLNESS INSIGHTS

Everyday ideas for feeling better.

Practical guidance for comfortable routines, mindful relaxation and responsible use of thermal wellness equipment.

◡
YOUR DAILY ROUTINE

Make room for 45 minutes of rest

Even a short break from a busy schedule creates an opportunity to slow down. Choose a consistent time, turn off distractions and focus on relaxation.

✳
HOME EQUIPMENT

Using thermal products comfortably

Follow the manufacturer's instructions, start with a comfortable temperature, and never fall asleep on a heating device unless its instructions explicitly allow it.

☼
WELLNESS & SAFETY

When to ask a clinician first

Seek professional advice before using heated massage equipment if you have reduced sensation, circulation problems, an implanted medical device or another relevant condition.
